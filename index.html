Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Dental Bonding and Porcelain Veneers?
Dental bonding uses a tooth-colored composite resin applied directly to the tooth in a single visit, making it ideal for minor fixes. Porcelain veneers are durable, custom-crafted ceramic shells designed in a lab for a longer-lasting, total smile transformation.
How long do Porcelain Veneers last?
With proper oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and basic care, porcelain veneers can last anywhere from 10 to 15 years or longer.
Why should I consider Invisalign Clear Aligners over traditional braces?
Invisalign clear aligners are nearly invisible, removable for easy eating and brushing, and engineered to straighten your teeth comfortably without metal wires or brackets.
Are Dental Implants a good permanent solution for missing teeth?
Yes, dental implants integrate directly with your jawbone, acting as a natural root replacement. They provide permanent support for crowns or bridges and prevent jawbone loss.
Is Professional Teeth Whitening safe for sensitive teeth?
Absolutely. Our professional teeth whitening treatments use clinically tested formulas administered or prescribed by dental professionals to minimize sensitivity while achieving optimal brightness.
